Three Ways to Classify Foods

By Origin

Foods of animal origin and foods of vegetable origin

By Chemical Composition

Proximate principles: proteins, fats, carbohydrates. Micronutrients: vitamins, minerals. Plus water and fibre.

By Function

Energy-yielding (cereals, fats, sugars), body-building (milk, meat, pulses, eggs), protective (vegetables, fruits, milk)

0:03–0:05. Nutritive value grouping (ICMR): cereals and millets; pulses and legumes; milk and meat products; fruits and vegetables; fats and sugars.

Proteins: Quality as Well as Quantity

  • Built from 20 amino acids; 9 are essential for adults
  • Quality: biological value, digestibility, net protein utilisation (NPU = BV × digestibility ÷ 100)
  • Egg protein is the reference protein
  • Best single marker of protein status: serum albumin (normal above 3.5 g/dL)
ICMR-NIN 2020

0.83 g/kg/day

Safe protein intake for Indian adults: about 54 g for the reference man (65 kg) and 46 g for the reference woman (55 kg).

0:05–0:09. Pregnancy adds 9.5 g (2nd trimester) and 22 g (3rd); lactation adds 17 g (0–6 months) and 13 g (7–12 months).

Fats: Which Fat Matters

Saturated

Ghee, butter, coconut and palm oil. Limit.

MUFA

Groundnut, mustard, olive, rice-bran oil.

PUFA

Linoleic (n-6) and alpha-linolenic (n-3) acids: the essential fatty acids. Fish, mustard, soybean, flaxseed.

Trans Fat

Vanaspati, bakery and fried snacks. FSSAI limit: 2% of total fat in oils and fats.

Total fat should be ≤ 30% of energy. Use a combination of oils rather than one oil.

0:11–0:15. Visible fats (oils, ghee) vs invisible fats (in cereals, pulses, nuts, milk). Essential fatty acid deficiency: phrynoderma (toad skin) is also linked to it.

Carbohydrates and Dietary Fibre

  • The main source of energy in Indian diets: 50–70% of energy comes from cereals
  • Prefer whole grains and millets to refined cereals
  • Glycaemic index: how fast a food raises blood glucose compared with glucose
Dietary Fibre

Not digested, but prevents constipation, lowers blood cholesterol, slows glucose absorption, and is linked to lower risk of colon cancer, diabetes and CHD. ICMR 2020 recommends roughly 30–40 g/day for adults, depending on energy intake.

0:15–0:18. Add sugar limit: ICMR-NIN advises less than 5% of energy from added sugar (about 25 g/day on a 2000 kcal diet). Verify against DGI 2024 before quoting.

Name the Deficiency

Fat-soluble: A, D, E, K. Water-soluble: B-complex and C (no body store worth relying on).

0:18–0:24. Pellagra's 3 Ds: dermatitis, diarrhoea, dementia; maize and jowar (excess leucine) diets. Minerals of public health importance in India: iron, iodine, calcium, zinc, fluorine (excess).

Requirement, Allowance and the Reference Indian

  • EAR (Estimated Average Requirement): meets the need of half the healthy population
  • RDA = EAR + 2 SD: meets the need of nearly all (97.5%)
  • Energy is given as the average requirement, with no safety margin: surplus energy becomes fat
  • TUL: the highest intake unlikely to cause harm
Reference Adults (19–39 y)

Man: 65 kg, 1.77 m, BMI 20.75
Woman: 55 kg, 1.62 m, BMI 20.95
8 h moderate work, 8 h in bed, 4–6 h sitting and moving, 2 h walking or active recreation

Diversity: The Cheapest Supplement

  • Source nutrients from at least 8 food groups a day
  • Pulses, meat and fish give only 6–9% of energy in Indian diets, against a recommended 14%
  • Cereals give 50–70% of energy, against a recommended maximum of 45%
The Double Burden

Take-Home

  1. Proteins, fats, carbohydrates give energy; vitamins and minerals protect.
  2. Protein: 0.83 g/kg/day; mix cereals and pulses.
  3. Fat ≤ 30% of energy; trans fat as near zero as possible.
  4. RDA = EAR + 2 SD, except energy (average, no margin).
  5. My Plate: half vegetables and fruits; eight food groups a day.
