TB Preventive Treatment (TPT)

Who Gets TPT
  • PLHIV (adults and children)
  • Household contacts of pulmonary TB: all ages (testing for infection where available for those 5 and above)
  • Other risk groups: on dialysis, anti-TNF, transplant, silicosis

Always rule out active TB first.

Regimens

6H: daily isoniazid for 6 months
3HP: weekly isoniazid + rifapentine for 3 months (12 doses)
For contacts of MDR-TB: levofloxacin (6Lfx)

Support That Keeps Patients on Treatment

Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana

₹1,000 a month by direct benefit transfer for the duration of treatment (raised from ₹500 in 2024)

Ni-kshay Mitra

Individuals and organisations adopt patients and give monthly food baskets

Ni-kshay Portal

Case notification, follow-up, payments, adherence technology

Treatment Supporter

A trained person close to the patient; honorarium on completion

Plus free diagnosis and drugs in public and private sectors, incentives for private providers to notify, and screening for HIV and diabetes in every TB patient.
