Day by Day: The Course of Measles

0:08–0:14. Koplik's spots: bluish-white spots on a red base on the buccal mucosa opposite the lower molars, pathognomonic, appear 1–2 days before the rash. The rash starts behind the ears and spreads downwards.

Why Measles Kills Children

Respiratory

Pneumonia (the commonest cause of death), otitis media, laryngotracheobronchitis

Gut and Nutrition

Diarrhoea; precipitates PEM and vitamin A deficiency (blindness)

Nervous System

Encephalitis; SSPE years later

Immune Amnesia

Measles wipes out existing immune memory for months to years

Treatment: vitamin A to every case (two doses, 24 hours apart), nutrition, treat complications.

0:14–0:20. Vitamin A doses by age: under 6 months 50,000 IU; 6–11 months 1 lakh IU; 12 months and above 2 lakh IU.

In an Outbreak

Vaccine within 72 hours of exposure can prevent measles. Immunoglobulin within 6 days for contacts who cannot be vaccinated.

India aims to eliminate measles and rubella; MR campaigns since 2017 covered children 9 months to 15 years.

0:27–0:33. Check the current national elimination target year (it has been revised more than once). Reconstituted measles vaccine left warm has caused deaths from toxic shock (contamination): a key safety teaching point.

Rubella: Mild in the Child, Cruel to the Foetus

  • Togavirus; incubation 2–3 weeks (about 18 days)
  • Mild fever, rash, post-auricular and sub-occipital lymphadenopathy
  • Infectious about a week before to a week after the rash

0:33–0:42. Classic CRS triad: cataract, sensorineural deafness, congenital heart disease (PDA). Also microcephaly, mental retardation, hepatosplenomegaly, "blueberry muffin" rash. Risk figures on the slide are approximate teaching values from Park and WHO; the earlier the infection, the higher the risk. Avoid pregnancy for 4 weeks after rubella vaccination.

Take-Home

  1. Measles: R₀ 12–18; stop it with about 95% immunity.
  2. Infectious 4 days before to 4 days after rash; Koplik's spots come first.
  3. Give vitamin A to every measles case.
  4. MR vaccine at 9–12 and 16–24 months; outbreak dose within 72 hours.
