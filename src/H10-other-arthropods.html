A Housefly Lands on Your Plate, Then Flies Off.

It was there for three seconds. What did it leave behind?

On its legs

Bristles and sticky pads carry organisms from wherever it last sat.

Vomit drop

It liquefies food with saliva and vomit before sucking it up.

Faeces

It defecates often, even while feeding.

0:00–0:03. These three are the routes of mechanical transmission by the housefly.

The Housefly: Musca domestica

  • Breeds in manure, garbage, nightsoil and decaying organic matter
  • Egg → larva (maggot) → pupa → adult in about a week in summer
  • Feeds on both filth and food: an ideal mechanical carrier
Diseases

Typhoid and paratyphoid, cholera, diarrhoea and dysentery, amoebiasis, polio, helminths, trachoma and conjunctivitis, anthrax, yaws; myiasis from larvae.

0:03–0:08.

Fly Control: Sanitation Comes First

1 · Most Important

Environmental Sanitation

Remove breeding places: refuse in covered bins, sanitary latrines, manure pits, clean animal sheds.

2

Insecticides

Residual sprays on resting surfaces; space sprays; baits. Resistance develops quickly.

3

Protect Food

Cover food; fly-proof kitchens, shops and eateries.

4

Health Education

Link flies to disease; promote clean surroundings.

0:08–0:13. Insecticides without sanitation give only temporary relief.

The Sandfly: Phlebotomus

  • Tiny (1.5–3.5 mm), hairy; wings held erect in a V
  • Breeds in cracks and crevices of mud walls, cattle sheds, moist soil rich in organic matter
  • Weak flier: hops; short flight range; bites at night
Diseases

Kala-azar (P. argentipes), oriental sore (cutaneous leishmaniasis), sandfly fever, Carrion's disease.

Control: indoor residual spraying of walls and cattle sheds up to about 6 ft (they rest low); plaster cracks; keep cattle sheds away from houses.

0:13–0:21. India's kala-azar elimination target: fewer than 1 case per 10,000 population at block level. Synthetic pyrethroids replaced DDT for IRS in kala-azar areas. Check the current elimination status before teaching.

The Body Louse Transmits

Epidemic typhus, relapsing fever, trench fever. Infection is by crushed lice or louse faeces rubbed into bites, not by the bite itself.

0:21–0:27. Control: personal hygiene, washing clothes in hot water, delousing with insecticide dusts in epidemics (refugee camps, disasters).

The Rat Flea: Xenopsylla cheopis

  • Wingless, laterally compressed, jumps; both sexes suck blood
  • X. cheopis: the most efficient vector of plague; also X. astia, X. brasiliensis
  • A "blocked flea": plague bacilli block the proventriculus; the hungry flea bites again and again, regurgitating bacilli
Diseases

Bubonic plague, endemic (murine) typhus, Hymenolepis diminuta.

0:27–0:31. Flea control must precede rat killing during a plague outbreak, or fleas leave dead rats and bite humans.

Calculate the Cheopis Index

Cheopis index = X. cheopis fleas ÷ rats examined

Average number of X. cheopis per rat. An index above 1 indicates a high risk of plague spread.

Cheopis Index

0:31–0:35. Other indices: general flea index (all fleas per rat), specific flea index, percentage of rats infested.

Ticks and Mites

Hard Ticks (Ixodidae)

Kyasanur Forest Disease (Haemaphysalis spinigera), Crimean-Congo haemorrhagic fever (Hyalomma), tick typhus, tularaemia, tick paralysis

Soft Ticks (Argasidae)

Endemic relapsing fever (Ornithodoros), Q fever

Trombiculid Mite

The larva ("chigger", Leptotrombidium deliense) transmits scrub typhus: look for the eschar.

Itch Mite

Sarcoptes scabiei: scabies, spread by close contact. House mouse mite: rickettsialpox.

0:35–0:45. Scrub typhus is now a leading cause of acute febrile illness and AES in many Indian states; doxycycline is the drug of choice. KFD: Shimoga district, Karnataka, and adjacent Western Ghats; a vaccine exists.

Cyclops: The Arthropod in the Step Well

  • Tiny freshwater crustacean (copepod), just visible to the naked eye
  • Intermediate host of guinea worm (Dracunculus medinensis) and the fish tapeworm (Diphyllobothrium latum)
Control
  • Physical: strain drinking water through cloth; boil; convert step wells to draw wells
  • Chemical: temephos; chlorine
  • Biological: larvivorous fish (barbel)

India was certified free of dracunculiasis in 2000.

Take-Home

  1. The housefly is a mechanical carrier: sanitation is the main control.
  2. Sandflies hop, rest low and transmit kala-azar: spray walls to about 6 ft.
  3. Body lice: epidemic typhus, relapsing fever, trench fever.
  4. X. cheopis carries plague; a cheopis index above 1 is a warning.
  5. Chiggers: scrub typhus; hard ticks: KFD; Cyclops: guinea worm.
