ICDS: Six Services, Who Gives Them and to Whom

0:09–0:15. Launched 2 October 1975 in 33 blocks. Three of six services are delivered by the health system: a classic example of convergence. Since 2021 ICDS is part of "Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0".

Take-Home

  1. ICDS (1975), now Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0: six services.
  2. Supplementary nutrition: 500 kcal for children, 600 kcal for mothers, 800 kcal for SAM.
  3. POSHAN Abhiyaan: 2–3% a year reductions, convergence, Poshan Tracker.

  5. Micronutrients: AMB, NIDDCP, vitamin A, fortified foods.
