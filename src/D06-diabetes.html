0:03–0:11. ICMR-INDIAB-17 (adults 20+, 2008–2020 data). Prevalence varies widely between states, highest in Goa, Puducherry and Kerala; the states with low prevalence now have high prediabetes, which predicts a future rise. Long-term effects: retinopathy, nephropathy, neuropathy, and higher risk of heart disease and stroke.

Which Type?

0:11–0:17. Type 1 incidence peaks at 10–14 years. Indians develop type 2 diabetes at younger ages and lower BMI than Europeans ("thin-fat" phenotype).

Read the Glucose Test

HbA1c 6.5% or more also diagnoses diabetes. Random glucose 200+ with symptoms is diagnostic.

Result

0:17–0:27. WHO: diabetes if fasting ≥126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L) or 2-hour ≥200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L). Impaired fasting glucose 110–125 (6.1–6.9); impaired glucose tolerance 2-hour 140–199 (7.8–11.0). Without symptoms, confirm with a repeat test. Pregnancy in India: single-step 75 g OGTT, 2-hour ≥140 mg/dL = GDM (national guideline, 2018).

Type 2 Diabetes Can Be Delayed

58%fewer new cases with lifestyle change in people with IGT (Diabetes Prevention Program, USA)
What Works
  • Lose 5–7% of body weight
  • 150 minutes of brisk walking a week
  • Less refined rice, sugar, sweet drinks; more fibre
  • No tobacco

Similar benefit was shown in Indian adults with IGT (Indian Diabetes Prevention Programme, Chennai).

0:37–0:45. Park notes there is no practical high-risk strategy for type 1 diabetes. For type 2, the population strategy (activity, diet, weight) and the high-risk strategy (screening and lifestyle for prediabetes) go together.
