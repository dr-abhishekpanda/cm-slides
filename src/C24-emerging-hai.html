0:00–0:03. Nipah virus, Kerala 2018: 18 confirmed cases (17 deaths per official counts; check NCDC/Kerala reports). Nurse Lini Puthussery died of infection acquired while caring for a patient. Rapid lab confirmation (NIV Pune), contact tracing and isolation contained it.

Emerging or Re-emerging?

Emerging

A disease that has appeared in a population for the first time, or existed before but is rapidly increasing in incidence or geographic range.

Nipah, Zika, COVID-19, mpox

Re-emerging

A known disease that had declined and is now returning as a public health problem.

Chikungunya (2006), plague (1994), diphtheria, drug-resistant TB

0:03–0:08. About three-quarters of emerging infections are zoonotic. Ask the class to name one more example of each.

The Big Four

An infection is hospital-acquired if it was not present or incubating at admission; usually appearing 48 hours or more after admission.

0:30–0:37. Common organisms in Indian ICUs: Klebsiella, Acinetobacter, Pseudomonas, E. coli, often carbapenem-resistant; Candida; MRSA.

Preventing HAI

My 5 Moments for Hand Hygiene
  1. Before touching a patient
  2. Before a clean or aseptic procedure
  3. After body fluid exposure risk
  4. After touching a patient
  5. After touching patient surroundings
And Then
  • Care bundles for each device
  • Standard and transmission-based precautions
  • Biomedical waste segregation
  • Infection control committee; surveillance
  • Kayakalp (2015): cleanliness and IPC awards

0:44–0:52. Alcohol hand rub for 20–30 seconds; soap and water for 40–60 seconds when hands are visibly soiled (and for C. difficile and norovirus). Remove catheters as soon as they are not needed: the simplest bundle element.

Antimicrobial Resistance

  • HAI and AMR feed each other: resistant organisms spread in wards
  • Drivers: over-the-counter antibiotics, misuse in animals, poor sanitation
  • Red Line campaign: antibiotics marked with a red vertical line need a prescription
  • Antimicrobial stewardship in every hospital
NAP-AMR 2.0

Launched 18 November 2025 for 2025–29: One Health approach across health, animal husbandry, agriculture, food and environment.
