A Soil Organism, Not Contagious

AgentClostridium tetani; spores survive years in soil; toxin tetanospasmin
SourceSoil, dust, animal and human faeces
EntryWounds, burns, unclean delivery and cord care, injections, ear infections
IncubationUsually 6–10 days (3–21)
No Herd Immunity

Tetanus does not spread person to person, so every individual must be immunised. Recovery does not give immunity.

0:03–0:10. Shorter incubation means more severe disease and higher fatality. Clinical: trismus, risus sardonicus, opisthotonus, spasms with full consciousness.

Neonatal Tetanus and MNTE

"Any neonate with a normal ability to suck and cry during the first 2 days of life, and who, between 3 and 28 days of age, cannot suck normally, and becomes stiff or has convulsions or both."
WHO surveillance case definition
Elimination Target

Fewer than 1 neonatal tetanus case per 1,000 live births in every district

India

MNTE validated in 2015, ahead of the global target

0:10–0:20. How India got there: Td/TT in pregnancy, institutional deliveries (JSY, JSSK), clean birth practices (the "cleans": hands, surface, blade, cord tie, cord care). Every neonatal tetanus case is still notified and investigated.
