She Stopped Cooking When the World Went White

A 68-year-old widow. Both pupils look grey. She can only count fingers at 1 metre. Her granddaughter left school to look after her.

Fifteen minutes of surgery could give her back her sight.
Why hasn't she had it?

0:00–0:03. Cataract: the leading cause of blindness in India, curable. Barriers: cost, distance, fear, no escort, belief that blindness is part of ageing. Note the social cost: the granddaughter's education.

0:03–0:12. Park: WHO defines blindness as "visual acuity of less than 3/60 (Snellen) or its equivalent". ICD-11 distance categories: mild (worse than 6/12), moderate (worse than 6/18), severe (worse than 6/60), blindness (worse than 3/60). Near vision impairment: worse than N6 at 40 cm. India adopted the WHO blindness definition (below 3/60) in 2017, replacing the older 6/60 cut-off.

0:36–0:48. Programme goal: reduce prevalence of blindness to 0.25% (check the current target). Vitamin A prophylaxis under RCH (1 lakh IU at 9 months, then 2 lakh IU every 6 months to 5 years) prevents nutritional blindness. Check component names against the current NPCBVI guidelines.

Refer and Follow

Vitamin A, measles vaccination, eye protection at work, no "home remedies" for eye injury
