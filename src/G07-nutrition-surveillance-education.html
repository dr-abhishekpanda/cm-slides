Nutritional Surveillance

"Watching over nutrition in order to make decisions that will lead to improvements in nutrition in populations."
WHO, 1976
Purpose 1

Describe the nutrition situation: who, where, how severe

Purpose 2

Give early warning of worsening (drought, floods, price rises)

Purpose 3

Plan, monitor and evaluate programmes

0:03–0:08. Surveillance is information for action: data collected and not used is not surveillance.

Indicators Worth Watching

Outcome
  • Stunting, wasting, underweight (under 5)
  • Low birth weight (< 2.5 kg)
  • Anaemia; goitre; night blindness
  • Adult BMI
Process
  • Exclusive breastfeeding
  • Minimum acceptable diet
  • IFA, vitamin A, deworming coverage
  • Iodised salt use
Early Warning
  • Rainfall, crop failure
  • Food prices
  • Migration, distress sale of assets
  • Disease outbreaks (measles, diarrhoea)

Growth Monitoring and Promotion

  • Weigh monthly up to 3 years, then quarterly
  • The direction of the line matters more than its position
  • Monitoring without promotion (counselling, action) is wasted effort

Nutrition Education That Works

  1. Find out what people eat and believe (24-hour recall, FGDs)
  2. Pick few, specific, doable messages
  3. Use local foods and recipes; cooking demonstrations
  4. Reach the decision-maker: mother-in-law, husband
  5. Use many channels: home visits, VHSNDs, schools, media
  6. Follow up and measure change
National Campaigns

Poshan Maah (September) and Poshan Pakhwada (March): Jan Andolan on nutrition under POSHAN Abhiyaan.

Nutrition Rehabilitation

Nutrition Rehabilitation Centres (NRCs)

Facility units, usually at district or block hospitals, for complicated SAM. Children stay about 14 days with their mothers; mothers get wage compensation and are trained in feeding and care.

The Idea Behind It

Rehabilitate the child and educate the mother using local foods, so that recovery continues at home. Follow-up visits after discharge.

Take-Home

  1. Surveillance = information for action.
  2. NFHS, CNNS, HMIS and Poshan Tracker are India's main data sources.
  3. Watch the direction of the growth line, then act.
  4. Respect food culture; reach the decision-maker.
  5. NRCs treat the child and teach the mother.
