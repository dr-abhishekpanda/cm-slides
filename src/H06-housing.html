Take-Home

  1. Housing = structure + surroundings + community services.
  2. Plinth 2–3 ft, roof ≥ 10 ft, windows 1/5 of floor area, daylight factor > 1%.
  3. At least two living rooms, a separate kitchen and a sanitary latrine.
  4. Overcrowding: persons per room, floor space and sex separation.
  5. Poor housing breeds TB, diarrhoea, scabies, ARI and mental stress.
