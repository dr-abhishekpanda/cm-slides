Heterosexual sex accounts for about 73% of new infections; injecting drug use about 11.5%. Rising infections among 15–24-year-olds and in some North-Eastern states.

How Risky Is Each Route?

0:10–0:18. Per-act figures are approximate. STIs with ulcers or discharge raise HIV transmission several-fold, which is why STI control is HIV prevention. Not spread by touching, sharing food, mosquitoes or toilet seats.

Testing: The Window and the Strategy

  • Window period: infected but antibody-negative; usually 3–12 weeks with current assays
  • Diagnosis in adults: three different tests (national strategy) at an ICTC
  • Infants under 18 months: DNA PCR (maternal antibodies confuse antibody tests)
  • Consent, confidentiality, counselling and referral: the "5 Cs"
Community-based Screening

A single reactive screening test is never a diagnosis: refer to an ICTC for confirmation.

0:18–0:25. The 5 Cs: consent, confidentiality, counselling, correct test results, connection (linkage) to care. The HIV and AIDS Act 2017 makes discrimination illegal and protects confidentiality.

The 95-95-95 Cascade

All PLHIV Virally Suppressed

0:25–0:35. "Treat All" since 2017: ART for every PLHIV regardless of CD4 count. First-line: TLD (tenofovir, lamivudine, dolutegravir). Annual viral load monitoring. Free ART at ART centres and link ART centres. Students are surprised that 95% x 95% x 95% is only about 86% of all PLHIV suppressed.

Prevent Parent-to-child Transmission

  1. Test every pregnant woman (HIV and syphilis) at the first ANC visit
  2. Lifelong ART from diagnosis, whatever the stage
  3. Institutional delivery; safe obstetric practices
  4. Infant nevirapine prophylaxis (longer, or dual, for high-risk infants)
  5. Exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months with mother on ART
  6. Early infant diagnosis: DNA PCR from 6 weeks; final antibody test at 18 months

0:35–0:42. Back to the opening case. India aims at elimination of vertical transmission of HIV and syphilis (EVTHS). Check the current infant prophylaxis duration and regimen in the NACO guidelines.
