What Is a Stroke?

"rapidly developed clinical signs of focal disturbance of cerebral function; lasting more than 24 hours or leading to death, with no apparent cause other than vascular origin"
WHO definition
Ischaemic

Thrombosis or embolism: the commonest type

Haemorrhagic

Intracerebral or subarachnoid bleeding

TIA

Same signs, gone within 24 hours: a warning

0:03–0:10. The 24-hour threshold excludes TIA. Cerebrovascular disease is a leading cause of NCD deaths and the leading cause of adult disability. Imaging now often shows infarcts even when symptoms resolve, so modern definitions of TIA rely on tissue findings.

Stroke: Who Is at Risk?

0:10–0:17. Controlling blood pressure in the population is the single biggest lever for stroke prevention. In sickle cell disease, stroke can occur in childhood (relevant in central and tribal India).

Rheumatic Fever: Not Infectious, but From an Infection

"Rheumatic fever is a febrile disease affecting connective tissues particularly in the heart and joints initiated by infection of the throat by group A β haemolytic streptococci."
Definition
Who

Children 5–15 years; poverty, crowding

Recurs

Each new strep throat can bring a new attack and more valve damage

Ends In

RHD (mitral valve most often); death often by 35

0:27–0:35. Park calls RHD "one of the most readily preventable" chronic diseases. Overcrowding, poor housing and lack of access to care for sore throat drive it, so it is a disease of poverty that has largely disappeared from high-income countries.

Diagnose It: Revised Jones Criteria (2015)

What Changed in 2015

Criteria differ for low-risk and moderate/high-risk populations. In high-risk settings like much of India, polyarthralgia or monoarthritis can count as major, and echocardiography is advised for every suspected case.

0:35–0:43. Low-risk population: acute RF incidence of 2 or fewer per 100,000 school-age children a year, or RHD prevalence of 1 or fewer per 1,000 at all ages (Park, citing the 2015 revision).

Primary and Secondary Prophylaxis

Primary: Treat the Sore Throat

Single injection of benzathine penicillin: 1.2 million units (adults), 600,000 units (children); or oral penicillin for 10 days.
