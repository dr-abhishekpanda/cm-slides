Why Have NCDs Overtaken Infections?

0:10–0:15. India carries a double burden: infections and undernutrition have not gone, while NCDs have risen. Park calls today's mortality the result of the "lag-time" effect of past exposure to risk factors.

Treat the Tail, or Shift the Curve?

Population measures: less salt, tobacco taxes, safe spaces to walk. Most heart attacks happen in people at moderate risk, simply because they are so many: the prevention paradox.

0:20–0:28. Park lists three approaches for CHD: population strategy, high-risk strategy and secondary prevention. Add primordial prevention: stopping risk factors from appearing at all, mainly in children (school food, physical activity, tobacco-free schools).

National Programme for Prevention and Control of NCDs

  • NPCDCS (2010) renamed NP-NCD in 2023; now also covers COPD, CKD, NAFLD, stroke and STEMI
  • Population-based screening of everyone aged 30+ at Ayushman Arogya Mandirs
  • NCD clinics at CHCs and district hospitals; cardiac and cancer care at higher levels
  • Health promotion; tracking on the National NCD Portal
