Why Children Become Malnourished

Immediate

Inadequate dietary intake and infection (diarrhoea, measles, ARI): a vicious cycle

Underlying

Household food insecurity; poor feeding and care practices (late weaning, diluted feeds); unsafe water, sanitation and health services

Basic

Poverty, women's education and status, large families with close birth spacing, food beliefs

Treating SAM: Ten Steps

Facility-based care in the Nutrition Rehabilitation Centre, then the community.

0:34.

The Ten Steps

Stabilisation (days 1–7)
  1. Treat/prevent hypoglycaemia
  2. Treat/prevent hypothermia
  3. Treat/prevent dehydration (ReSoMal, slowly)
  4. Correct electrolyte imbalance
  5. Treat infection (antibiotics for all)
  6. Correct micronutrient deficiencies (no iron yet)
  7. Start cautious feeding: F-75
Rehabilitation (weeks 2–6)
  1. Achieve catch-up growth: F-100, then family foods
  2. Provide sensory stimulation and emotional support
  3. Prepare for follow-up after discharge

Target weight gain in rehabilitation: about > 5 g/kg/day (good: > 10). Discharge when oedema has gone and weight is adequate (e.g. 15% gain).

0:34–0:44. Iron is started only in rehabilitation. Check discharge criteria against your state's NRC guideline.

Community-based Management (CMAM)

Identify

Anganwadi workers and ASHAs measure weight, height and MUAC; enter on Poshan Tracker

Treat at Home

Uncomplicated SAM with good appetite: medical check-up, appetite test, extra take-home ration, counselling, weekly follow-up

Refer

Complications, failed appetite test, oedema or under 6 months: NRC

0:44–0:48. Verify the current protocol name and steps with your District Programme Officer (ICDS).

Preventing PEM

  • Exclusive breastfeeding for 6 months; adequate complementary feeding from 6 months
  • Growth monitoring and promotion
  • Immunisation; early treatment of diarrhoea (ORS, zinc) and ARI
  • Deworming, vitamin A, iron and folic acid
  • Supplementary nutrition: ICDS, PM-POSHAN
  • Birth spacing; women's education
  • Safe water and sanitation
  • Food security: Public Distribution System
