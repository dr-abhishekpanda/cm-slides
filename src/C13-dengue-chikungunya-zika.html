Know the Mosquito
  • Day biter, early morning and late afternoon
  • Breeds in clean, stored water: coolers, tyres, pots
  • Short flight range
  • Eggs survive months when dry; transovarial transmission

A second infection with a different serotype raises the risk of severe dengue.

0:07–0:15. Antibody-dependent enhancement explains part of the risk with secondary infection. Urbanisation, water storage, and solid waste (tyres, containers) drive transmission. Post-monsoon peak.

Spot the Warning Signs

Dengue without warning signs, with warning signs, and severe dengue (shock, severe bleeding, organ failure).

0:15–0:22. Any warning sign means admission and close monitoring. Avoid NSAIDs and aspirin; use paracetamol. Prophylactic platelet transfusion is not indicated in stable patients.

Which Test, Which Day?

Rapid card tests are useful for screening; the programme confirms by ELISA at sentinel surveillance hospitals.

0:22–0:27. RT-PCR in the first days also detects the serotype. A positive NS1 or IgM ELISA confirms a case for surveillance purposes per NCVBDC.

Survey the Ward: Stegomyia Indices

House Index

% houses positive

Breteau Index

positive containers per 100 houses

0:27–0:37. Container index = positive containers / containers inspected x 100. Rules of thumb used by the programme: house index above 10% or Breteau index above 20 suggests high risk of transmission; Breteau index above 50 signals high outbreak risk, and below 5 low risk. Check NCVBDC guidance before using for decisions.

Controlling Aedes

Source Reduction

Empty and scrub containers weekly (dry day); cover tanks; remove tyres and junk

Larvicide

Temephos in stored water that cannot be emptied; larvivorous fish in ornamental tanks

Adults and People

Space spraying in outbreaks; full sleeves, repellents; screens; community action

Fogging alone does not control dengue: larvae in containers keep producing adults.

0:37–0:45. Urban bye-laws, construction-site inspection and school campaigns. Wolbachia-infected mosquito release is being studied in several countries, including India (ICMR).

Take-Home

  1. Aedes: day biter, clean stored water.
  2. Danger comes as the fever falls: watch warning signs.
  3. NS1 days 1–5; IgM after day 5.
  4. House and Breteau indices guide action.
  5. Weekly dry day beats fogging.
