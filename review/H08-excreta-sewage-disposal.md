# Review Notes · H08 Excreta and Sewage Disposal

**Competencies:** CM 3.4

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Your Drive copy of Park 28e Chapter 15 (145 MB) returned no text through the Drive connector, so this deck uses the 27e chapter. Please check the verbatim quotes and figures against 28e.
- **RCA latrine dimensions (page 6).** The water seal of about 2 cm, the 75 cm pit diameter and the roughly 3 m depth are from my knowledge of Park and the RCA design. The extract I could read only confirmed the 30-inch pit and the 90 cm squatting plate. Please verify against Park.
- **Twin-pit design (page 7).** Pit life of about 3–5 years and a rest period of about 2 years are commonly cited SBM(G) figures. Verify against the current SBM(G) technical note.
- **One gram of faeces (page 2).** These are advocacy figures, labelled in the notes as illustrative orders of magnitude.
- **STP standards (page 10).** The deck gives the historic Royal Commission 20/30 standard. Current CPCB discharge norms are stricter. Add them if you wish.
