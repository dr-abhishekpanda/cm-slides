# Review Notes · G06 Breastfeeding and Complementary Feeding

**Competencies:** CM 5.12, 5.13, 5.16–5.18, 5.22

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Park 28e Chapter 11 (111 MB) is too large for the Drive connector, so this deck uses the 27e Nutrition chapter, which already carries the ICMR-NIN 2020 requirements. Check quotes and figures against 28e.
- **NFHS figures.** These are NFHS-5 (2019–21) national figures. Add your state's figures, and switch to NFHS-6 once its national report is out.
- **NFHS-5 IYCF figures (pages 3 and 12).** Early initiation is 41.8%, exclusive breastfeeding 63.7%, and minimum acceptable diet about 11%. Verify all three in the NFHS-5 India report.
- **Breast-milk storage times (page 7).** These vary between guidelines. Use your state's.
- **Energy-gap chart (page 9).** The bar heights are schematic. The kcal values are approximate WHO/PAHO figures.
