## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Park 28e Chapter 11 (111 MB) is too large for the Drive connector, so this deck uses the 27e Nutrition chapter, which already carries the ICMR-NIN 2020 requirements. Check quotes and figures against 28e.

- **Gomez and IAP cut-offs (page 6).** These are given from standard teaching. Verify them against Park 28e.
- **CMAM protocol (page 11).** I refer to the 2023 MoWCD–MoHFW protocol under Poshan 2.0. Check the current name and steps with your District Programme Officer.
- **NRC discharge criteria (page 10).** These vary between state guidelines.
