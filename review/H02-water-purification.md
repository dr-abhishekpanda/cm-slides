# Review Notes · H02 Purification of Water

**Competencies:** CM 3.2, CM 3.3

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Your Drive copy of Park 28e Chapter 15 (145 MB) returned no text through the Drive connector, so this deck uses the 27e chapter. Please check the verbatim quotes and figures against 28e.
- **Orthotolidine (page 15).** Park still describes the OT and OTA tests. I added a one-line note that orthotolidine is restricted in many countries and that DPD kits are the common field alternative. Delete it if your department teaches OT/OTA only.
- **pH slider (page 12).** The curve assumes a pKa of about 7.5 for hypochlorous acid. At pH 8.5 it gives about 90% ionised, which matches Park. The other values on the slider are calculated, not taken from Park.
- **Well calculator (page 19).** It uses Park's rough rule of 2.5 g bleaching powder per 1,000 L. Field practice should start from the chlorine demand measured with Horrock's apparatus.
