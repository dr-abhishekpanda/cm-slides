# Review Notes · H04 Air Pollution and Ventilation

**Competencies:** CM 3.1

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Your Drive copy of Park 28e Chapter 15 (145 MB) returned no text through the Drive connector, so this deck uses the 27e chapter. Please check the verbatim quotes and figures against 28e.
- **PM2.5 standards (page 5).** WHO 2021 gives 5 µg/m³ (annual) and 15 (24-hour). India's NAAQS 2009 gives 40 and 60. Check whether CPCB has revised NAAQS since.
- **AQI bands (page 12).** These follow CPCB's National AQI (2014). The health statements are paraphrased.
- **NCAP target (page 11).** The speaker notes give the revised target of up to 40% reduction by 2025–26. Verify it on the PRANA portal.
- **Inversion animation (page 6).** This is a schematic animation, not a model.
