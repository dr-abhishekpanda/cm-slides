# Review Notes · H12 Biomedical Waste Management

**Competencies:** CM 14.1–14.4

## Check Before Teaching

- **Main source.** Park 28e Chapter 16 (the Drive connector read this chapter) and the BMW Management Rules 2016 as amended in 2018 and 2019.
- **Schedule I details (pages 6–7).** These follow the amended Rules and the CPCB 2018 guidelines. Check whether any amendment after 2019 changes the categories.
- **Incinerator temperatures (page 10).** 800 ± 50 °C and 1050 ± 50 °C with a 2-second residence time come from the Schedule II emission standards.
- **Symbols (page 13).** These are simplified drawings. Show the official Schedule IV labels from the CPCB guideline in class.
- **Needle-stick case (page 2).** Check the PEP timings in the notes against the current NACO PEP guidelines.
