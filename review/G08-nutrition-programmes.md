# Review Notes · G08 National Nutrition Policy and Programmes

**Competencies:** CM 5.6

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Park 28e Chapter 11 (111 MB) is too large for the Drive connector, so this deck uses the 27e Nutrition chapter, which already carries the ICMR-NIN 2020 requirements. Check quotes and figures against 28e.
- **Supplementary nutrition norms (page 5).** These are the Poshan 2.0 norms (2023). Verify them against the current Gazette notification, because older Park editions give different numbers.
- **Fortified rice (page 3).** The rollout is given as complete by March 2024. Verify on PIB.
- **PM-POSHAN norms (page 7).** 450 kcal and 12 g protein for primary, 700 kcal and 20 g for upper primary.
