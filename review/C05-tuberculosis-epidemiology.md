# Review Notes · C05 Tuberculosis: Epidemiology and Diagnosis

**Competencies:** CM 8.1, CM 8.3

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** This deck draws on Park 27e Chapter 5 Part I (respiratory infections), read in full. Park 28e chapter files are too large for the Drive connector; check quotes and figures against 28e.
- **Burden figures (page 3).** WHO Global TB Report 2025 as reported in November 2025 (incidence 187 per lakh, deaths 21 per lakh, 26.18 lakh notified). Update from the India TB Report each year.
- **Adult BCG (page 10 notes).** Adult BCG was used in some states in 2024; check its current status.
- **NAAT-first algorithm (page 9).** Check against the latest Central TB Division diagnostic algorithm.
