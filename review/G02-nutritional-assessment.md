# Review Notes · G02 Assessing Nutritional Status

**Competencies:** CM 5.2, CM 5.9

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Park 28e Chapter 11 (111 MB) is too large for the Drive connector, so this deck uses the 27e Nutrition chapter, which already carries the ICMR-NIN 2020 requirements. Check quotes and figures against 28e.
- **Growth chart (page 7).** The curves are smoothed approximations of the WHO 2006 boys' weight-for-age median, −2 SD and −3 SD. They are fine for teaching, not for clinical plotting.
- **MUAC (page 8).** The SAM/MAM cut-offs are WHO 2009. The notes also give Park's older age-independent cut-offs.
- **Biochemical cut-offs (page 10).** Check them against the WHO 2020 ferritin and WHO 2024 haemoglobin guidelines.
