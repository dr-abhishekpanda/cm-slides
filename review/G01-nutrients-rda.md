# Review Notes · G01 Nutrients, Requirements and My Plate

**Competencies:** CM 5.1, CM 5.11

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Park 28e Chapter 11 (111 MB) is too large for the Drive connector, so this deck uses the 27e Nutrition chapter, which already carries the ICMR-NIN 2020 requirements. Check quotes and figures against 28e.
- **RDA table (page 12).** Values come from ICMR-NIN 2020 as reproduced in Park 27e Table 28. The text layer of that table was scrambled in places. I cross-checked iron, calcium, iodine, zinc, vitamin A, folate and vitamin D, but please verify every cell against the printed table.
- **Dietary fibre (page 7).** I give a range of 30–40 g/day instead of one figure, because the scrambled table made the per-activity values unclear.
- **Added sugar (page 7 notes).** The notes give less than 5% of energy. Confirm this against DGI 2024.
- **My Plate (page 14).** Quantities are verified from your Drive copy of ICMR-NIN DGI 2024, Table 1.2a.
