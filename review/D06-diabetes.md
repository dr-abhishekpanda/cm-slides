# Review Notes · D06 Diabetes Mellitus

**Competencies:** CM 8.2

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** This deck uses Park 27e Chapter 6, read in full from the split 27e set in your Drive. Quotations in quotation marks are from that text. Check them against 28e if you teach from it.
- **Glucose classifier (page 5).** WHO cut-offs from Park: diabetes FPG 126+ or 2-hour 200+; IFG 110–125; IGT 140–199.
- **IDRS (page 6).** Checked against published reproductions of Mohan et al. 2005: age 0/20/30, waist 0/10/20 (women 80/90, men 90/100 cm), physical activity 0/20/30 (exercise plus strenuous work / either / neither), family history 0/10/20; maximum 100; 60 or more is high risk. Some later papers use a modified activity scale (0/10/20/30); the deck follows the original.
- **DPP 58% (page 8).** From Knowler et al., NEJM 2002.
