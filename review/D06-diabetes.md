## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** This deck uses Park 27e Chapter 6, read in full from the split 27e set in your Drive. Quotations in quotation marks are from that text. Check them against 28e if you teach from it.
- **Glucose classifier (page 5).** WHO cut-offs from Park: diabetes FPG 126+ or 2-hour 200+; IFG 110–125; IGT 140–199.
- **IDRS (page 6).** Scored from the original Mohan 2005 paper as I recall it: age 0/20/30, waist 0/10/20, activity 0/20/30, family history 0/10/20; 60+ high risk. **Check against the paper before teaching.**
- **DPP 58% (page 8).** From Knowler et al., NEJM 2002.
