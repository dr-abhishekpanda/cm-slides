# Review Notes · E01 National Health Programmes and the NHM

**Competencies:** CM 8.3, CM 17.5

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** This deck uses Park 27e Chapter 7 (National Health Mission, NRHM, NUHM, Ayushman Bharat), read in full from the split 27e set in your Drive. Check quotations against 28e if you teach from it.
- **Scope.** Health care delivery (PHC, CHC, district, AAM staffing), health planning and PM-JAY in depth belong to Part K (K5–K10). This deck gives the programme overview and the NHM framework only, so there is little overlap.
- **NRHM launch date (page 7 notes).** Park says 5 April 2005; other government sources say 12 April 2005. The slide says only "2005" and "April 2005" in the notes.
- **NHM continuation (page 10 notes).** The Union Cabinet reviewed NHM (2021–24) in January 2025 and approved its continuation; most press reports say five years, one says two. Check the PIB release.
- **Financing (page 10).** Centre : State 60 : 40 (90 : 10 for North-Eastern and Himalayan states) since 2015. Flexible pools were merged from 2022–23 into one pool for RCH, health systems, national programmes and urban health; state PIPs may still show the older pool names.
- **ASHA numbers (page 8 notes).** "About 10 lakh" is not confirmed from a primary source in this session. Take the figure from the latest NHSRC ASHA update.
- **Ayushman Bharat figures (page 12).** 1.86 lakh Ayushman Arogya Mandirs and 96.43 crore ABHA IDs as of 12 August 2026 (PIB factsheet); PM-ABHIM ₹64,180 crore (2021–26); PM-JAY ₹5 lakh per family per year, extended to everyone aged 70+ in October 2024 (Vay Vandana). Update before each teaching cycle.
- **Targets scorecard (page 14).** NHM targets from Park Table 10; status from SRS 2022 (IMR 26, TFR 2.0), MMR Special Bulletin 2019–21 (93), kala-azar (2023), leprosy (PIB, October 2025: 0.57 per 10,000; 638 districts below 1) and WHO Global TB Report 2025.
- **Opening case (page 2).** Programme names are current as of November 2026 (NTEP, NCVBDC, NP-NCD, NPCBVI).
