# Review Notes · H01 Safe and Wholesome Water

**Competencies:** CM 3.1, CM 3.2 · **Pages:** 22 · **Main source:** Park 27e, Environment and Health (Water)

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Your Drive copy of Park 28e Chapter 15 is 145 MB, and the Drive connector returned no text for it. I used the 27e chapter (`[27e]Park_Ch15.pdf`). Please check the verbatim quotes on pages 4 and 9, and the sanitary-well figures on page 12, against 28e.
- **Jal Jeevan Mission figures (page 3).** These are about 17% coverage in 2019 and about 81% (15.75 crore of 19.36 crore households) by 27 Nov 2025. They come from Ministry of Jal Shakti statements as reported in the press. Confirm them on ejalshakti.gov.in before teaching.
- **WHO guideline values (page 17).** Fluoride 1.5 mg/L, nitrate 50 mg/L and arsenic 0.01 mg/L are from WHO GDWQ 4th ed., as quoted in Park. The Indian standard, IS 10500:2012, is covered in H03.
- **Water-related disease groups (page 16).** These follow Park's classification. Park does not use the water-washed and water-based labels from Bradley's classification. If your students meet those terms in other textbooks, mention them in the speaker notes.
