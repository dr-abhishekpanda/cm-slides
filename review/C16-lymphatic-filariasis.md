# Review Notes · C16 Lymphatic Filariasis

**Competencies:** CM 8.1, CM 8.3, CM 3.6

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Checked against Park 27e Chapter 5 Parts II–VII (the split 27e set in your Drive). An earlier version of this note wrongly said Park could not be read. No conflicts with Park were found in the facts checked (incubation periods, key figures, schedules). Check against 28e if you teach from it.
- **Burden (page 3).** 348 endemic districts in 20 states/UTs; 143 had stopped MDA (2025 national review in press/PIB). Update from NCVBDC.
- **MDA dosing (page 7 notes).** DEC age bands and ivermectin height bands: check the current NCVBDC dosing chart.
- **65% effective coverage (calculator, page 8).** WHO GPELF threshold of the total population; some programmes use 85% of the eligible population.
