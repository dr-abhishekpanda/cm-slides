# Review Notes · H03 Water Quality, Hardness and Conservation

**Competencies:** CM 3.2

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Your Drive copy of Park 28e Chapter 15 (145 MB) returned no text through the Drive connector, so this deck uses the 27e chapter. Please check the verbatim quotes and figures against 28e.
- **IS 10500:2012 limits (page 5).** I give pH, turbidity, TDS, hardness, fluoride, nitrate, arsenic and E. coli. Check these against the current BIS text, because some parameters were changed by later amendments. I left out iron for that reason.
- **Jal Jeevan Mission surveillance (page 11).** This says five women per village are trained with field test kits, as set out in the 2021 JJM framework. Add current numbers from ejalshakti.gov.in if you want them.
- **Opening case (pages 2 and 6).** It uses an invented borewell report. Swap in a real report from your district laboratory, anonymised.
