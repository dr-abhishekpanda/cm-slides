# Review Notes · H05 Noise, Radiation and Heat

**Competencies:** CM 3.1

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Your Drive copy of Park 28e Chapter 15 (145 MB) returned no text through the Drive connector, so this deck uses the 27e chapter. Please check the verbatim quotes and figures against 28e.
- **Safe listening times (page 4).** These use the NIOSH rule: 85 dB(A) for 8 hours, halving for every 3 dB more. Park only says 85 dB per day is the limit. Indian factory rules use a different exchange rate, so the speaker notes call this an illustration.
- **Noise Rules 2000 (page 6).** The standards and the silence-zone definition are from the Schedule. States may allow limited festival relaxation.
- **Radiation dose limits (page 12).** These are ICRP 103 values: 20 mSv/year occupational, 1 mSv/year public. Park quotes older ICRP genetic-dose wording.
- **Heat illness (page 14).** Mild-to-severe categories follow NCDC's National Action Plan on Heat Related Illnesses. Check whether the plan has been updated.
