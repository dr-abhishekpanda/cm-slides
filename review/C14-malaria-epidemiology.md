# Review Notes · C14 Malaria: Epidemiology

**Competencies:** CM 8.1, CM 3.7

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Checked against Park 27e Chapter 5 Parts II–VII (the split 27e set in your Drive). An earlier version of this note wrongly said Park could not be read. **Corrections made:** P. vivax incubation changed to Park's 8–17 days (mean 14). Check against 28e if you teach from it.
- **Two different 2024 case numbers (page 3).** Programme-reported (~2.27 lakh, from a secondary summary of the NCVBDC/ICMR-NIMR report) and WHO-estimated (~20.7 lakh, WMR 2025). Confirm the reported figure on the NCVBDC website.
- **Pf/Pv share (page 4).** Deliberately not given; insert your state's figures.
- **Indicator calculator (page 9).** ABER target of 10% is the classic surveillance norm; check whether your programme uses a different benchmark in elimination settings.
