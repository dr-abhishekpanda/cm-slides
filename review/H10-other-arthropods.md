# Review Notes · H10 Flies, Fleas, Lice, Ticks, Mites and Cyclops

**Competencies:** CM 3.6, CM 3.7

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** Your Drive copy of Park 28e Chapter 15 (145 MB) returned no text through the Drive connector, so this deck uses the 27e chapter. Please check the verbatim quotes and figures against 28e.
- **Missing photos.** The Commons housefly and itch-mite files I picked were GFDL-licensed, which I did not accept. The rat flea and tick downloads were blocked by Wikimedia's rate limit. Those pages show a grey placeholder. Add your own images to `img/` with the names shown, plus a `.json` credit file, then run `python3 tools/build.py H10`.
- **Rat-flea diseases (page 6).** These follow Park. Park lists chiggerosis under fleas, but that is the sand flea (Tunga), not the rat flea.
- **Kala-azar target (page 5 notes).** Elimination is defined as fewer than 1 case per 10,000 population per block. Check the current status before teaching.
