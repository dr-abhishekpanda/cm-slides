# Review Notes · D04 Stroke and Rheumatic Heart Disease

**Competencies:** CM 8.2

## Check Before Teaching

- **Park edition.** This deck uses Park 27e Chapter 6, read in full from the split 27e set in your Drive. Quotations in quotation marks are from that text. Check them against 28e if you teach from it.
- **Jones criteria (page 8).** From the 2015 AHA revision (summarised in Park). Check the major/minor lists for moderate/high-risk populations.
- **Secondary prophylaxis durations (page 9).** WHO/AHA durations; Park follows the same. Three-weekly dosing in India.
- **Thrombolysis window (page 5).** 4.5 hours is the standard; check the NP-NCD stroke protocol.
