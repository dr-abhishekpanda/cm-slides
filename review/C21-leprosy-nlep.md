## Check Before Teaching

- **Park text not read for this deck.** Park 27e Chapter 5 Part II (polio onwards) is over the Drive connector's 10 MB limit, and 28e is larger still. The textbook content in this deck is standard and was written from established sources, but **nothing is quoted from Park**, and every footer citing "Park 27e, Ch 5" should be checked against your copy (27e or 28e) before teaching. If you can upload Part II as smaller split PDFs, I can recheck it and add short quotations.
- **2024–25 indicators (page 3).** Prevalence 0.57 per 10,000 (PIB, October 2025). Child proportion (4.68% vs 5.18%) and grade 2 disability (1.8% vs 2.17%) differ between PIB and other sources; the slide uses PIB.
- **Classification and three-drug PB regimen (pages 6–7).** From the DGHS letter of 6 March 2025 (effective 1 April 2025). I did not read the annexure; check the exact nerve criteria and child doses there.
- **Notifiability.** Some reports describe a move to make leprosy notifiable; not stated on the slides. Add if confirmed.
