Bacterial Zoonoses · Plague

How Plague Reaches People

  1. Wild rodents (Tatera indica) keep the bacillus in burrows
  2. Spills over to domestic rats; rats die ("rat fall")
  3. Hungry rat flea Xenopsylla cheopis bites humans → bubonic
  4. Spread to lungs → pneumonic: person-to-person droplets
Control Order Matters

Kill fleas first (insecticide in burrows and houses), then rats. Killing rats first sends fleas to people.

Contacts: chemoprophylaxis with doxycycline or tetracycline; watch for 7 days.

0:08–0:20. Incubation: bubonic 2–7 days; pneumonic 1–3 days. Flea index: X. cheopis index above 1 signals risk of an outbreak. Indian events: Surat and Beed (1994); Hatkoti, Himachal Pradesh (2002); Dangud, Uttarakhand (2004). Plague is notifiable under IHR. Natural foci persist in parts of Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra and the Himalayan belt.

Bacterial Zoonoses · Leptospirosis

Who Gets Leptospirosis?

ReservoirRats (lifelong urinary shedding); cattle, pigs, dogs
RouteContact with contaminated water or soil through cuts, soaked skin, eyes, mouth
IncubationAbout 10 days (2–30)
Severe formWeil's disease: jaundice, renal failure, bleeding; pulmonary haemorrhage
At Risk

Paddy farmers, sewer and sanitation workers, fishermen, veterinarians, abattoir workers; everyone after floods.

0:20–0:28. Endemic states include Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Goa and Andaman and Nicobar. Clues: conjunctival suffusion, calf tenderness. Diagnosis: IgM ELISA; MAT is the reference test.

NCDC Leptospirosis guidelines; Park 27e, Ch 5

Bacterial Zoonoses · Scrub Typhus

Scrub Typhus: Look for the Eschar

  • Larval trombiculid mite (chigger) of Leptotrombidium bites humans in scrub, grass, field edges
  • Rising cause of acute undifferentiated fever and AES in India; post-monsoon
  • Eschar: painless black scab in skin folds (axilla, groin, waist)
  • Complications: pneumonitis, myocarditis, meningoencephalitis, ARDS
Diagnose and Treat

IgM ELISA (Weil-Felix OX-K is insensitive). Doxycycline; azithromycin in pregnancy and children. Do not wait for tests in a sick patient.

0:34–0:44. Teach students to look for the eschar in every febrile patient: undress and examine the folds. Prevention: clear scrub around homes, full clothing, repellents, avoid sitting on grass.

ICMR Rickettsial Guidelines 2015; Park 27e, Ch 5
Bacterial Zoonoses · Brucellosis

Brucellosis: The Undulant Fever

Species

B. melitensis (goats, sheep: most severe), B. abortus (cattle), B. suis (pigs)

Spread

Raw milk and milk products; handling placenta and aborted fetuses; lab aerosols

Prevent

Boil or pasteurise milk; gloves at births; vaccinate calves (national programme)

Long, waxing and waning fever with sweats, joint pain and fatigue. Doxycycline plus rifampicin for 6 weeks.

0:44–0:52. Occupational: farmers, veterinarians, butchers, dairy workers, lab staff. India's National Animal Disease Control Programme vaccinates female calves aged 4–8 months against brucellosis.
