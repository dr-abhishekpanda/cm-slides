Health Programmes and NHM · Opening Case

One Household, How Many Programmes?

Tap each member of the family. Which programme reaches them, and who brings it to the door?

0:00–0:04. Six or more national programmes meet in one home, mostly through the same three people: the ASHA, the ANM and the Community Health Officer. That convergence is what the National Health Mission was designed to achieve. Ask students to add programmes they think are missing (for example, NVHCP testing in pregnancy, IDSP if someone has fever with rash).

PM-ABHIM and ABDM (2021); NP-NCD and Sickle Cell Elimination Mission (2023)

0:04–0:09. The arc runs from single-disease campaigns to an integrated mission and then to comprehensive primary health care. Many programmes were renamed as goals changed: NMCP → NMEP (1958) → NVBDCP → NCVBDC; NLCP → NLEP (1983); NTP → RNTCP (1997) → NTEP (2020); NPCB → NPCBVI (2017); NPCDCS → NP-NCD (2023).

Health Programmes and NHM · NHM

The NHM Umbrella

0:20–0:25. Park: "many different programmes have been brought together under the overarching umbrella of National Health Mission (NHM), with National Rural Health Mission (NRHM) and National Urban Health Mission (NUHM) as its two sub-Missions." NRHM began in April 2005 with a special focus on 18 states (8 Empowered Action Group states, 8 North-Eastern states, Himachal Pradesh and Jammu and Kashmir). Park also credits NHM with reducing out-of-pocket spending from 72% to 60%.

Health Programmes and NHM · NRHM Platforms

What NRHM Built

0:25–0:31. India's ASHAs received the WHO Director-General's Global Health Leaders Award in May 2022. There are about 10 lakh ASHAs (check the latest NHSRC ASHA update). Park: 5.55 lakh VHSNCs (2021) and 33,757 Rogi Kalyan Samitis (June 2021). The district is the core unit of planning, budgeting and implementation.

Health Programmes and NHM · NUHM

The Urban Arm: NUHM

  • All state capitals, district headquarters and cities or towns of 50,000 and above
  • Focus: slum dwellers, the homeless, rag-pickers, street children, construction and brick-kiln workers, migrants
  • Urban PHCs and urban CHCs; ANMs for outreach
  • Urban ASHA for 1,000–2,500 urban poor (200–500 households)
Mahila Arogya Samiti

A women's group for 50–100 households in a slum, with an annual grant: the urban counterpart of the VHSNC.

0:31–0:34. Park: the seven largest metropolitan cities run NUHM directly through their municipal corporations. Urban health also depends on sanitation, water and vector control by urban local bodies, so NUHM strengthens their public health capacity.

Health Programmes and NHM · Governance and Money

Who Decides, Who Pays?

  1. Centre: Mission Steering Group (Union Health Minister); Empowered Programme Committee (Union Health Secretary)
  2. State: State Health Mission and State Health Society
  3. District: District Health Society: the unit of planning
  4. Village and ward: VHSNC, Mahila Arogya Samiti, Jan Arogya Samiti
Money

Centre : State = 60 : 40 (90 : 10 in North-Eastern and Himalayan states).

Each state sends a yearly Programme Implementation Plan; funds come through flexible pools, merged since 2022–23 into one pool for RCH, health systems, national programmes and urban health.

0:34–0:40. The fund-sharing ratio changed to 60:40 in 2015 (earlier 75:25). Older documents and many state PIPs still show separate pools: RCH flexipool, NRHM mission flexipool, NUHM flexipool, communicable disease pool, NCD pool and infrastructure maintenance. In January 2025 the Union Cabinet reviewed NHM (2021–24) and approved its continuation; press reports differ on the period (five years in most). Check the PIB release.

Health Programmes and NHM · Ayushman Bharat

The Pillars of Ayushman Bharat

1.86 lakhAyushman Arogya Mandirs (renamed from Health and Wellness Centres) functional by August 2026
₹5 lakhPM-JAY: hospital cover per family per year; all people aged 70+ added in 2024
₹64,180 crPM-ABHIM (2021–26): critical-care blocks, labs, surveillance
96 croreABHA health IDs under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (August 2026)

0:40–0:45. Announced February 2018; the first Health and Wellness Centre opened in Bijapur, Chhattisgarh, on 14 April 2018. The sub-centre-level team is led by a Community Health Officer (BSc Nursing, GNM or Ayurveda graduate with a six-month community health certificate), with MPWs and ASHAs. PM-JAY covers about 12 crore families (55 crore people); the Vay Vandana expansion for everyone aged 70 and above came in October 2024. Figures: PIB factsheet, 12 August 2026. Update before teaching.

Health Programmes and NHM · Service Packages

Which of the 12 Service Packages?

0:45–0:51. The twelve packages (Park): 1 care in pregnancy and childbirth; 2 neonatal and infant care; 3 childhood and adolescent care; 4 family planning, contraceptive and other reproductive care; 5 management of communicable diseases (national health programmes); 6 general outpatient care for acute simple illnesses and minor ailments; 7 screening, prevention, control and management of NCDs and chronic communicable diseases such as TB and leprosy; 8 basic oral health; 9 mental health; 10 common ophthalmic and ENT problems; 11 elderly and palliative care; 12 emergency medical services including burns and trauma.
