The JE Transmission Cycle

0:03–0:12. Case fatality of JE encephalitis is 20–30%, and up to half of survivors have lasting neurological sequelae. Most cases are children under 15. Endemic belts: Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Assam, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and others. Incubation 5–15 days.

JE, AES and KFD · AES

Acute Encephalitis Syndrome

"A person of any age, at any time of year, with the acute onset of fever and a change in mental status (including symptoms such as confusion, disorientation, coma, or inability to talk) and/or new onset of seizures (excluding simple febrile seizures)."
WHO / NCVBDC clinical case definition

AES is a syndrome, not a disease. JE is only one of its causes.

0:12–0:17. The syndromic definition allows surveillance to capture all cases before laboratory confirmation. Samples: serum and CSF for JE IgM ELISA at sentinel sites.

NCVBDC: JE/AES guidelines (AES case definition)
JE, AES and KFD · Causes of AES

Many Roads to AES

0:17–0:25. Litchi-associated AES (Muzaffarpur): unripe litchi contains hypoglycin A and MCPG; children who skipped the evening meal developed hypoglycaemia. Prevention: ensure an evening meal, limit litchi, correct glucose early. Scrub typhus was found to cause many AES cases in Gorakhpur; empirical doxycycline or azithromycin is now part of AES protocols.

JE Vaccine in Endemic Districts

JE-19–12 months, with MR-1
JE-216–24 months, with MR-2
CampaignChildren 1–15 years at introduction; adults in selected high-burden districts
VaccinesLive attenuated SA 14-14-2; inactivated (JENVAC, indigenous)
Beyond the Vaccine
  • Pigsties away from homes
  • Mosquito nets and repellents
  • Intermittent irrigation of paddy
  • Early referral; AES treatment centres

0:25–0:35. Fogging has limited effect on Culex in paddy fields. Pig vaccination is not practical in India. Check which districts in your state are JE-endemic and covered.

JE, AES and KFD · KFD Epidemiology

KFD: Ticks, Monkeys and the Forest

First seen1957, Shimoga (Shivamogga), Karnataka: dead monkeys in the forest
AgentFlavivirus
VectorHard tick Haemaphysalis spinigera
HostsSmall mammals (reservoir); monkeys amplify and die
SeasonNovember to June, peak in the dry months
Who Gets It

People entering the forest: cattle grazers, firewood and leaf-litter collectors, forest workers. Now reported from Kerala, Goa, Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu too.

0:35–0:43. Clinical: sudden fever, headache, myalgia, haemorrhage; a second phase with meningoencephalitis in some. Case fatality 2–10%. A dead monkey in the forest is a sentinel event: search the area and dust it with insecticide.

Park 27e, Ch 5: Kyasanur Forest Disease; ICMR

Preventing KFD

JE, AES and KFD · Take-Home

Take-Home

  1. JE: birds → Culex → pigs → humans (dead end).
  2. AES is a syndrome: test for JE, scrub typhus and others.
  3. JE vaccine at 9 and 16–24 months in endemic districts.
  4. KFD: tick-borne; dead monkeys are the alarm.
  5. Forest workers: repellent, clothing, tick checks.
