What Causes Cancer?

0:11–0:21. Park: smoking causes about 71% of lung cancer globally. Areca nut (supari, gutka) is itself carcinogenic even without tobacco: a major cause of oral submucous fibrosis and oral cancer in India.

Cancer · Danger Signals

Teach the Public the Danger Signals

A lump or hard area in the breast

A change in a wart or mole

Persistent change in digestive or bowel habits

Persistent cough or hoarseness

Bleeding between periods or from any natural orifice

A swelling or sore that does not heal; unexplained weight loss

0:21–0:26. Park lists eight danger signals (two are combined here). These are the backbone of health education for early diagnosis.

Cancer · Cancer Control

Cancer Control

"Cancer control consists of a series of measures based on present medical knowledge in the fields of prevention, detection, diagnosis, treatment, after care and rehabilitation…"
Definition
Primary

Tobacco control (COTPA), HPV and hepatitis B vaccines, healthy diet and weight, occupational safety

Secondary

Screening and early diagnosis

Tertiary

Treatment, rehabilitation and palliative care

0:26–0:32. HPV vaccine: the national HPV vaccination campaign was launched on 28 February 2026 (PIB): a free, voluntary single dose of Gardasil-4 for girls aged 14 (about 1.15 crore), with parental consent, at government facilities; girls turning 15 within the 90-day intensive phase are also eligible, after which it continues on routine immunisation days. The indigenous Cervavac was not used in the national drive. Check for any extension to other age groups.

Cancer · Screening

Screen Three Cancers, Everyone 30–65

Screen-positives are referred for confirmation. Screening without assured follow-up does harm.

0:32–0:42. Mammography is not used for population screening in India because of cost and denser breast tissue in younger women; HPV DNA testing is being introduced in some states. Screening interval for oral and breast in the framework is every 5 years; check the current NP-NCD guidance.

MoHFW Operational Framework 2016; NP-NCD; Park 27e, Ch 6

Cancer · VIA

How VIA Works

  1. Prepare 5% acetic acid fresh: 5 mL glacial acetic acid in 95 mL distilled water
  2. Apply to the cervix with a speculum and good light; wait 1 minute
  3. Dense, well-defined acetowhite area near the squamocolumnar junction = positive
  4. Positive: treat (cryotherapy or thermal ablation) or refer for colposcopy

Cheap, immediate, trainable: sensitivity similar to cytology. A "screen and treat" visit can finish care in one sitting.

0:42–0:48. WHO's 90-70-90 targets for cervical cancer elimination by 2030: 90% of girls HPV-vaccinated by 15, 70% of women screened with a high-performance test by 35 and 45, and 90% of those with disease treated.

Cancer · Registries

Count to Control: Cancer Registries

Hospital-based

Cases seen in a hospital: useful for treatment and survival patterns, not for rates

Population-based

All cases in a defined area: gives incidence rates. Ideal population: 2–7 million

India's National Cancer Registry Programme (ICMR-NCDIR, since 1981) runs a network of population- and hospital-based registries.
