A Steep Fall, But Not Gone

~2.27 lakhmalaria cases reported by the programme in 2024 (11.7 lakh in 2015)
~20.7 lakhcases estimated by WHO for India in 2024
2024India left WHO's High Burden to High Impact group

Why two numbers? Programme figures count tested and reported cases; WHO models cases missed by the system, including the private sector.

0:03–0:10. India still accounts for most cases in the WHO South-East Asia Region (about 73% of estimated cases in 2024, per WMR 2025). The 2024 reported figure comes from a secondary summary of the NCVBDC/ICMR-NIMR report; confirm from the NCVBDC website. Most burden is in tribal, forested and border districts; Odisha, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Meghalaya and Mizoram have historically reported large shares.

Malaria Epidemiology · Life Cycle

Two Hosts, One Parasite

0:16–0:25. Control points: drugs act on blood stages; primaquine kills hypnozoites (radical cure of vivax) and falciparum gametocytes (stops transmission). The extrinsic cycle shortens in warm, humid weather, which explains the post-monsoon peak.

Malaria Epidemiology · Indian Vectors

Match the Terrain to the Vector

0:25–0:35. Anopheles bite at night, rest with the body at an angle to the surface, and breed in clean or slightly polluted water depending on the species. An. stephensi has spread to the Horn of Africa, a global concern. Back to the opening case: An. fluviatilis (or An. minimus in the North-East).

Malaria Epidemiology · Host and Environment

Who Gets Malaria, Where, and When?

Host

Young children and pregnant women in high-transmission areas; non-immune migrants and travellers. Sickle cell trait and G6PD deficiency give some protection.

Environment

Temperature 20–30 °C, humidity over 60%, rainfall creating pools. Post-monsoon peak.

Human-made

Irrigation, dams, mining pits, construction, water storage, deforestation, migration.

0:35–0:40. Ask students to name one human-made breeding site in their own area. Mining districts often report high malaria because of pits and migrant workers.

Malaria Epidemiology · Malariometric Indicators

Measure It: ABER, API, SPR

ABER %
API per 1000
SPR %
SfR %

0:40–0:52. ABER = smears examined / population x 100 (expected at least 10% a year, so that surveillance is adequate). API = confirmed cases / population x 1000. SPR = positives / smears examined x 100. SfR = falciparum positives / smears examined x 100. API is valid only when ABER is adequate.

Take-Home

  1. Cases have fallen sharply; India left HBHI in 2024.
  2. India: falciparum and vivax; vivax relapses.
  3. culicifacies rural, stephensi urban, fluviatilis and minimus in hills.
