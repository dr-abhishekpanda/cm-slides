The Hookworm Cycle

0:12–0:20. Each A. duodenale worm takes about 0.15–0.2 mL of blood a day; N. americanus about 0.03 mL. Heavy infection in children and pregnant women causes significant iron-deficiency anaemia. Prevention: toilets, footwear, deworming.

Intestinal Worms · National Deworming Day

National Deworming Day

  • Since 2015; rounds on 10 February and 10 August
  • Children and adolescents 1–19 years, in schools and anganwadis
  • Out-of-school children reached through anganwadis
  • Mop-up day about a week later for those missed
  • Teachers and anganwadi workers give the tablet

0:26–0:35. Pregnant women receive albendazole after the first trimester through ANC (Anemia Mukt Bharat). Under Anemia Mukt Bharat, biannual deworming also covers children through NDD.

Intestinal Worms · Guinea Worm

How a Step-well Spread a Metre-long Worm

  1. Person drinks water with Cyclops carrying larvae
  2. Female worm grows for about a year
  3. Painful blister, usually on the leg; person soaks it in water
  4. Worm releases larvae; Cyclops eat them
How India Did It
  • Convert step-wells to draw-wells; hand pumps
  • Filter drinking water through cloth
  • Temephos in ponds to kill Cyclops
  • Case containment

Last case 1996; certified free in 2000.

0:35–0:45. Dracunculiasis was mainly in Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh. Globally only a handful of human cases remain each year, mostly in Chad and South Sudan, with animal infections (dogs) in Chad complicating eradication. Check the latest WHO WER summary.
