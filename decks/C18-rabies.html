Rabies · Opening Case

A Street Puppy Nipped His Hand

A 10-year-old with two small bleeding tooth marks. His mother applied chilli paste and waited three days. "It was only a puppy."

Is it too late? What does he need today?

0:00–0:03. Never too late while the person is well: wash the wound, give rabies immunoglobulin into the wound and start vaccine. Bleeding bite = Category III. Rabies is almost 100% fatal once symptoms begin and almost 100% preventable before.

Rabies · Burden

100% Fatal, 100% Preventable

~36%of the world's rabies deaths occur in India (WHO estimate, about 20,000 a year)
5,726rabies deaths a year by ICMR-NIE's 2022–23 national survey
96%of human cases follow dog bites

Estimates differ because WHO's figure rests on older modelling. Either way, many deaths go unreported.

0:03–0:08. ICMR-NIE also estimated over 9 million animal bites a year. Surveillance reported far fewer deaths (about 54 in 2024), which shows under-reporting. The "96% dog" figure is commonly cited by NCDC; check the current NRCP materials.

Rabies · Epidemiology

Virus, Source, Spread

AgentRabies virus (Lyssavirus); bullet-shaped
SourceSaliva of rabid dogs, cats, monkeys, jackals, mongooses; bats elsewhere
SpreadBite, scratch, lick on broken skin or mucosa
IncubationUsually 1–3 months; days to over a year
Shorter Incubation When

Bites are severe, multiple, or on the head, face, neck, hands: richly innervated sites closer to the brain.

0:08–0:13. The virus travels along nerves to the brain, then to the salivary glands. Rodent bites (rats, squirrels) and rabbit bites do not usually need PEP in India per national guidelines.

Park 27e, Ch 5: Rabies; NCDC 2019
Rabies · Categorise the Exposure

Which Category?

0:13–0:25. Category is decided by the exposure, not the animal's vaccination status. If the biting dog or cat stays healthy for 10 days, the vaccine course can be changed to PrEP-equivalent per guidelines; never delay starting PEP while you wait.

NCDC National Guidelines for Rabies Prophylaxis, 2019; WHO 2018

Rabies · Wound Care

First Aid That Saves Lives

  1. Wash under running water with soap for 15 minutes
  2. Apply an antiseptic: povidone-iodine or alcohol
  3. Do not suture; if unavoidable, delay and infiltrate RIG first
  4. No chilli, turmeric, oil or any local application
  5. Tetanus prophylaxis and antibiotics if needed

0:25–0:31. Washing alone greatly reduces the virus load at the site. Back to the opening case: the chilli paste delays and irritates; wash even now.

NCDC National Guidelines for Rabies Prophylaxis, 2019

Rabies · RIG Calculator

Rabies Immunoglobulin for Category III

  • Give on day 0 (up to day 7 of the vaccine course)
  • Infiltrate into and around all wounds
  • Do not inject the remainder intramuscularly
  • Dilute if wounds are many
Equine RIG · 40 IU/kg

Human RIG · 20 IU/kg

0:40–0:46. Rabies monoclonal antibodies are licensed in India as an alternative; dose per the product. Skin testing before ERIG is no longer required (WHO 2018); be prepared for anaphylaxis. Typical concentrations: ERIG 300 IU/mL, HRIG 150 IU/mL; check the vial.

Rabies · PrEP and Elimination

Before Exposure, and Towards Zero

Pre-exposure Prophylaxis

Days 0, 7 and 21 or 28 for vets, dog handlers, lab staff, health workers in anti-rabies clinics, and children in high-risk areas.

National Action Plan, 2021
  • Zero human deaths from dog-mediated rabies by 2030
  • Mass dog vaccination (70% coverage)
  • Free PEP; animal birth control
  • One Health: health and animal husbandry together

0:46–0:54. The plan was launched on World Rabies Day, 28 September 2021. Vaccinating 70% of dogs interrupts transmission in the dog population, which is the only route to elimination; PEP alone protects individuals.

NCDC 2019; National Action Plan for Dog Mediated Rabies Elimination, 2021

Rabies · Take-Home

Take-Home

  1. Wash 15 minutes with soap and running water.
  2. Category decides: I nothing, II vaccine, III RIG + vaccine.
  3. IM 0-3-7-14-28 or ID 0-3-7-28 (2 sites).
  4. RIG into the wound, day 0.
  5. Elimination needs dog vaccination.
