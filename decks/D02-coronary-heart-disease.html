Coronary Heart Disease · Definition

What Is CHD?

"impairment of heart function due to inadequate blood flow to the heart compared to its needs, caused by obstructive changes in the coronary circulation to the heart"
WHO definition
Presents As

Angina, myocardial infarction, arrhythmia, heart failure, sudden death

Modern Epidemic

A disease of populations, "not an unavoidable attribute of ageing"

In India

Cardiovascular disease: about 27% of all deaths

0:03–0:10. Myocardial infarction is specific to CHD; angina and sudden death are not. Many first events are fatal, so prevention matters more than treatment. CHD in India strikes at younger ages than in Western populations.

Coronary Heart Disease · Population Strategy

Population Strategy: Start With the Plate

"CHD is primarily a mass disease. The strategy should therefore be based on mass approach focusing mainly on the control of underlying causes (risk factors) in whole populations, not merely in individuals."
On the population strategy
Fat

20–30% of energy; saturated fat under 10%

Cholesterol

Under 100 mg per 1,000 kcal a day

Salt

5 g a day or less; more complex carbohydrates

0:25–0:33. Park lists the WHO Expert Committee dietary changes, then smoking control, physical activity, BP control and health education. A smoke-free society, not a "safer cigarette", is the goal.

Park 27e, Ch 6: Prevention of CHD (WHO Expert Committee)
Coronary Heart Disease · Four Levels

Four Levels of Prevention

  1. Primordial: stop risk factors appearing in children: school meals, play, tobacco-free schools
  2. Population: shift the whole community's risk: salt, tobacco, activity
  3. High-risk: find and treat individuals with high total risk (screening 30+)
  4. Secondary: after a first event: quit tobacco, aspirin, statin, BP and sugar control, rehabilitation

0:33–0:42. Secondary prevention gives the largest individual benefit; population and primordial strategies give the largest public health benefit. Back to the case: the father needs secondary prevention; the sons need primordial and primary prevention.

Coronary Heart Disease · Acute Care

When It Happens: Time Is Muscle

Recognise

Chest pain over 20 minutes, sweating, breathlessness: call 108 at once

First Aid

Chewable aspirin; CPR and AED for cardiac arrest

Hub and Spoke

ECG and thrombolysis at district hospitals; PCI at hub centres

STEMI care has been added under NP-NCD. Community CPR training saves lives in sudden cardiac arrest.
