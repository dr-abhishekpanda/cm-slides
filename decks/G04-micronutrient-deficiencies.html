Why call it "hidden" hunger, and why does it matter?

0:00–0:03. Hidden hunger: micronutrient deficiency without obvious hunger or wasting. Effects: lower IQ and school performance, lower work capacity, maternal deaths, low birth weight, infections.

Grade the Anaemia

0:07–0:12. Cut-offs (g/dL), WHO 2011: children 6–59 m and pregnant women: anaemia <11, mild 10–10.9, moderate 7–9.9, severe <7. Non-pregnant women: <12; mild 11–11.9, moderate 8–10.9, severe <8. Anaemia Mukt Bharat: 6 × 6 × 6

6 Target Groups

Children 6–59 m · 5–9 y · adolescents 10–19 y · women of reproductive age · pregnant women · lactating mothers

6 Interventions

Prophylactic IFA · deworming · year-round behaviour change communication · testing and treatment (digital Hb) · fortified foods in public programmes · addressing non-nutritional causes (malaria, haemoglobinopathies, fluorosis)

6 Institutional Mechanisms

Inter-ministerial coordination · national centre of excellence · convergence · supply chain · dashboard · AMB scorecard

0:12–0:16. Launched 2018 under POSHAN Abhiyaan. Target: 3 percentage points' reduction in anaemia prevalence per year.

Micronutrient Deficiencies · IDD Spectrum

Iodine Deficiency Disorders Across Life

Life stageDisorders
FoetusAbortion, stillbirth, congenital anomalies, neurological cretinism, perinatal death
NeonateNeonatal goitre, neonatal hypothyroidism
Child and adolescentGoitre, impaired mental function, retarded physical development
AdultGoitre and its complications, hypothyroidism, impaired mental function

Goitre is only the tip: the main burden is lost intellectual potential in whole communities.

0:22–0:27. Goitrogens: cabbage, cauliflower, cassava (thiocyanates) add to deficiency.

Park 27e, Ch 11: Iodine deficiency disorders (spectrum by life stage)
Micronutrient Deficiencies · NIDDCP

National Iodine Deficiency Disorders Control Programme

  • Started 1962 as the National Goitre Control Programme; renamed NIDDCP in 1992
  • Universal salt iodisation; sale of non-iodised salt for human use is banned
  • Iodine content: ≥ 30 ppm at production, ≥ 15 ppm at consumer level
  • Surveys, urinary iodine and salt monitoring (salt testing kits with ASHAs)
IDD Is a Public Health Problem When
  • Total goitre rate in school children ≥ 5%
  • Median urinary iodine < 100 µg/L
  • Fewer than 90% of households use adequately iodised salt

0:27–0:34. Double-fortified salt (iodine + iron) is also supplied through some public programmes.

MoHFW, NIDDCP revised guidelines; FSS Regulations on iodised salt

Micronutrient Deficiencies · Xerophthalmia

WHO Classification of Xerophthalmia

Night blindness is the earliest symptom; X2 and X3 are emergencies that can blind within days.

0:34–0:40. Public health problem if (2–5 y): XN > 1%, X1B > 0.5%, X2+X3A+X3B > 0.01%, XS > 0.05%, or serum retinol < 10 µg/dL in > 5%.

Park 27e, Ch 11: Xerophthalmia (WHO classification)

Micronutrient Deficiencies · Vitamin A Prophylaxis

Vitamin A: Prevent and Treat

Prophylaxis (UIP)

1 lakh IU at 9 months, with MR vaccine.
2 lakh IU every 6 months from 16–18 months up to 5 years.
Total: 9 doses.

Treatment of Xerophthalmia

Over 1 year: 2 lakh IU orally on day 0, day 1 and day 14 (or at any worsening).
6–12 months: half the dose. Under 6 months: a quarter.

Also: breastfeeding, green leafy vegetables, yellow and orange fruits, measles vaccination, and treating diarrhoea.

0:40–0:48. Vitamin A supplementation also reduces childhood mortality, particularly from measles and diarrhoea. Verify the schedule against the current UIP handbook.

MoHFW, Vitamin A supplementation schedule (Universal Immunization Programme)
Micronutrient Deficiencies · Zinc and Others

Other Micronutrients to Remember

Zinc

Growth faltering, diarrhoea, poor wound healing. Zinc for 14 days in every child with diarrhoea: 10 mg/day under 6 months, 20 mg/day above.

Vitamin D

Rickets and osteomalacia, and widespread low levels in Indian adults despite sunshine.

Folate and B12

Megaloblastic anaemia; folate before conception prevents neural tube defects.
