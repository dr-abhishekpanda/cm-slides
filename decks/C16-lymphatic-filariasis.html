Lymphatic Filariasis · Agent and Vector

Two Worms, Two Kinds of Mosquito

Share in IndiaOver 99%Pockets (Kerala, coastal belts)
VectorCulex quinquefasciatusMansonia

Nocturnal Periodicity

Microfilariae appear in peripheral blood at night, matching the night-biting Culex. Blood is collected after 8.30 pm.

0:08–0:15. Humans are the only reservoir for W. bancrofti. Transmission is inefficient: many infective bites over years are needed, which is why it clusters in areas with poor drainage and sanitation. Incubation (to microfilaraemia) is long, often 8–16 months or more.

Lymphatic Filariasis · Clinical Spectrum

From Silent Infection to Elephantiasis

  1. Asymptomatic microfilaraemia: the largest group; hidden lymphatic damage
  2. Acute attacks: fever, painful red limb (adenolymphangitis), often with skin bacterial infection
  3. Chronic: lymphoedema and elephantiasis of legs; hydrocele in men
  4. Others: chyluria; tropical pulmonary eosinophilia

Diagnosis: night blood smear; filariasis test strip (antigen, any time of day) for surveys.

0:15–0:23. Repeated acute attacks drive progression of lymphoedema, which is why limb hygiene matters so much.

Lymphatic Filariasis · MDA

Mass Drug Administration

  • Once a year to the whole eligible population of an endemic district
  • Swallowed in front of the drug administrator
  • Needs over 65% coverage of the total population, year after year
  • National rounds from 10 February and 10 August

0:23–0:35. DEC 6 mg/kg (by age bands: 2–5 years 100 mg, 6–14 years 200 mg, 15 years and above 300 mg); albendazole 400 mg; ivermectin by height. Ivermectin is not given under 5 years or below 90 cm height. Check the current NCVBDC dosing chart. In IDA districts, more than two rounds have been needed where coverage was low.

NCVBDC: Morbidity Management and Disability Prevention
Lymphatic Filariasis · Stopping MDA

When Can a District Stop?

  1. At least 5 rounds of effective MDA (fewer with IDA)
  2. Microfilaria rate below 1% in sentinel and spot-check sites
  3. TAS-1 in children aged 6–7 years (antigen or antibody test): pass → stop MDA
  4. TAS-2 and TAS-3 at 2–3 year intervals to confirm no resurgence

Vector control (source reduction, drains, larvicides) supports MDA, especially in towns.

0:48–0:53. Children born after MDA started should have no infection if transmission has stopped; that is why TAS tests 6–7-year-olds.

Lymphatic Filariasis · Take-Home

Take-Home

  1. W. bancrofti + Culex; night blood after 8.30 pm.
  2. MDA: DA or IDA, swallowed in front of the worker.
  3. Not for under-2s, pregnant women, the seriously ill.
  4. MMDP: limb care and hydrocele surgery.
  5. Stop MDA only after passing TAS; target 2027.
