Housing · Space Standards

Space Inside the House

≥ 2living rooms; at least one can be closed for security
120 sq ftminimum living room for more than one person (100 for one)
50–100sq ft per person: minimum 50, optimum 100
500–1,000cu ft of air space per person (at least 500, preferably 1,000)
Windows

At least 2 per living room; area 1/5 of floor; doors + windows 2/5; sill not above 3 ft.

Lighting

Daylight factor above 1% over half the floor area.

Services

Separate kitchen, sanitary privy, bathing place, safe water, daily refuse removal.

0:16–0:20. Rural standards may be lower: at least two living rooms, ample verandah, built-up area not over one-third of the plot, separate kitchen, sanitary latrine, window area at least 10% of floor area.

3 · Sex Separation

Overcrowding exists if two persons over 9 years, of opposite sexes and not husband and wife, must sleep in the same room.

Housing · Take-Home

Take-Home

  1. Housing = structure + surroundings + community services.
  2. Plinth 2–3 ft, roof ≥ 10 ft, windows 1/5 of floor area, daylight factor > 1%.
  3. At least two living rooms, a separate kitchen and a sanitary latrine.
  4. Overcrowding: persons per room, floor space and sex separation.
  5. Poor housing breeds TB, diarrhoea, scabies, ARI and mental stress.
