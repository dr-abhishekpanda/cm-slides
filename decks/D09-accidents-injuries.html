Accidents and Injuries · Definition

Not So Accidental

"Accidents represent a major epidemic of non-communicable disease in the present century. They are no longer considered accidental."
On accidents
Definition

"An unexpected, unplanned occurrence which may involve injury" (WHO, 1956: "unpremeditated event resulting in recognizable damage").

Epidemiological View

Injuries have agents (energy), hosts and environments, and follow patterns: they are predictable and preventable.

0:03–0:08. Many prefer "crash" or "injury" to "accident", which implies chance. Unintentional injuries (road, falls, drowning, burns, poisoning) vs intentional (self-harm, violence).

Accidents and Injuries · Four Es

Prevention: The Four Es

Education

Road safety in schools; driver training; awareness of drink-driving and speed

Enforcement

Helmets, seat belts, speed limits, drink-driving checks; higher fines (MV Amendment Act 2019)

Engineering

Safer roads and junctions, black-spot correction, footpaths, safer vehicles (airbags, ABS)

Emergency Care

Ambulance network, trauma centres, first-responder training

0:30–0:40. Engineering and enforcement change behaviour more reliably than education alone. A correctly worn helmet reduces the risk of death by roughly 40% and serious head injury by about 70% (WHO estimates).

MoRTH road safety policy; Park 27e, Ch 6: Prevention of accidents
