Tetanus and Yaws · Epidemiology

A Soil Organism, Not Contagious

AgentClostridium tetani; spores survive years in soil; toxin tetanospasmin
SourceSoil, dust, animal and human faeces
EntryWounds, burns, unclean delivery and cord care, injections, ear infections
IncubationUsually 6–10 days (3–21)
No Herd Immunity

Tetanus does not spread person to person, so every individual must be immunised. Recovery does not give immunity.

0:03–0:10. Shorter incubation means more severe disease and higher fatality. Clinical: trismus, risus sardonicus, opisthotonus, spasms with full consciousness.

Park 27e, Ch 5: Tetanus
Tetanus and Yaws · Neonatal Tetanus

Neonatal Tetanus and MNTE

"Any neonate with a normal ability to suck and cry during the first 2 days of life, and who, between 3 and 28 days of age, cannot suck normally, and becomes stiff or has convulsions or both."
WHO surveillance case definition
Elimination Target

Fewer than 1 neonatal tetanus case per 1,000 live births in every district

India

MNTE validated in 2015, ahead of the global target

0:10–0:20. How India got there: Td/TT in pregnancy, institutional deliveries (JSY, JSSK), clean birth practices (the "cleans": hands, surface, blade, cord tie, cord care). Every neonatal tetanus case is still notified and investigated.

WHO; MoHFW MNTE validation (2015)

Td Across a Lifetime

WhoDoses
InfantsPentavalent at 6, 10, 14 weeks
ChildrenDPT boosters at 16–24 months and 5–6 years
AdolescentsTd at 10 and 16 years
Pregnant womenTd-1 early; Td-2 4 weeks later; Td booster if 2 doses received in a pregnancy within the last 3 years

0:20–0:28. Td replaced TT in 2018 to also protect against diphtheria. Td-2 must be given at least 4 weeks before delivery for maximum protection of the newborn.

MoHFW Td operational guidelines 2018; UIP

Choose both.

0:28–0:40. Tetanus-prone: over 6 hours old, puncture, devitalised tissue, contaminated with soil or faeces, burns, compound fractures. Simplified from the standard table: class 1 (within 5 years): nothing; class 2 (5–10 years): clean, nothing; tetanus-prone, one booster; class 3 (over 10 years): one booster; tetanus-prone, booster (immunoglobulin if very contaminated per some guidelines); class 4: full course, and for tetanus-prone also human tetanus immunoglobulin 250 IU IM at a different site. Tetanus and Yaws · Yaws

How India Ended Yaws

AgentTreponema pallidum subsp. pertenue
SpreadSkin-to-skin contact; children; non-venereal
WhereRemote tribal hill and forest areas: Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand
Lesions"Mother yaw" (papilloma), then skin and bone lesions
  1. 1996: Yaws Eradication Programme
  2. House-to-house search; benzathine penicillin to cases and contacts
  3. Last case 2003; declared free 2006
  4. WHO certified India yaws-free in 2016

0:40–0:52. Today single-dose oral azithromycin is the WHO treatment of choice (total community treatment in the Morges strategy). Surveillance for yaws continues alongside leprosy and skin camps in former endemic tribal districts.

Park 27e, Ch 5: Yaws; WHO SEARO (2016)
