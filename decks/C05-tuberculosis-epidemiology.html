Tuberculosis · Agent and Transmission

How TB Spreads

AgentM. tuberculosis (also M. bovis): acid-fast, slow-growing
SourceMainly sputum-positive pulmonary cases; also cattle (bovine)
SpreadDroplet nuclei in the air, from coughing, sneezing, talking
IncubationWeeks to years; infection to primary lesion 3–6 weeks
Infectiousness

An untreated sputum-positive patient infects about 10–15 people a year. Infectivity drops sharply within 2 weeks of effective treatment.

0:09–0:15. Droplet nuclei are 1–5 µm and stay airborne for hours; ventilation and sunlight reduce risk. Extrapulmonary TB is rarely infectious.

Tuberculosis · Presumptive TB

Who Is a Presumptive TB Patient?

Pulmonary

Any of: cough for 2 weeks or more, fever for 2 weeks or more, significant weight loss, haemoptysis, or any abnormality on chest X-ray.

Any Duration

In vulnerable groups (PLHIV, diabetes, contacts, malnourished, elderly, prisoners, miners), any cough or symptom counts.

Case finding: passive (people who come to the clinic) and active (house-to-house searches in high-risk groups).

0:31–0:37. Screening for presumptive TB in the community often uses the 4-symptom screen: cough, fever, weight loss, night sweats.

Central TB Division, NTEP: definition of presumptive TB
Tuberculosis · Diagnosis

The Diagnostic Pathway

  1. Sputum for NAAT (CBNAAT or Truenat) upfront for all presumptive TB: detects TB and rifampicin resistance in hours
  2. Where NAAT is not available: sputum smear microscopy, and send sample to a NAAT site
  3. Rifampicin-resistant: test further (line probe assay, liquid culture) and start DR-TB treatment
  4. Chest X-ray as a sensitive screen; clinical diagnosis if tests are negative but suspicion high
  5. Notify every case on Ni-kshay (mandatory, public and private)

0:37–0:45. Universal drug susceptibility testing (UDST) means every diagnosed patient should get a rifampicin-resistance test. TB notification is mandatory (since 2012); private sector included.

Tuberculosis · Tuberculin Test and BCG

Tuberculin Test and BCG

Mantoux (Tuberculin) Test

5 TU PPD (or 2 TU RT-23) intradermally; read induration at 48–72 hours. ≥ 10 mm positive. Shows infection, not disease; false negatives in malnutrition, HIV, measles. IGRA is the blood-test alternative.

BCG

Live attenuated M. bovis; at birth (up to 1 year), 0.1 ml (0.05 ml under 1 month) intradermal, left upper arm. Protects against severe childhood TB (miliary, meningitis), much less against adult pulmonary TB.

0:45–0:50. BCG reaction: papule at 2–3 weeks, ulcer, scar by 6–12 weeks. Adult BCG vaccination was tried in some states in 2024 as part of the 100-day campaign: check the current status.

Tuberculosis · Take-Home

Take-Home

  1. India: about a quarter of the world's TB; incidence 187/lakh (2024).
  2. Spread by droplet nuclei from sputum-positive cases.
  3. Only 5–10% of the infected fall ill; undernutrition and HIV tip the balance.
  4. Presumptive TB: cough ≥ 2 weeks, or any symptom in vulnerable groups.
  5. NAAT upfront; notify every case on Ni-kshay.
