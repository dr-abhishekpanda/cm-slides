Malaria Elimination · The Framework

From Control to Elimination

  • 1953: National Malaria Control Programme; 1958: Eradication Programme
  • 1965: cases fell to about 1 lakh; then resurgence (6.4 million in 1976)
  • 1977: Modified Plan of Operation
  • 2016: National Framework for Malaria Elimination 2016–2030
  • NSP 2023–27: zero indigenous cases by 2027; elimination by 2030
Lesson of the 1970s

Insecticide and drug resistance, weak surveillance and funding gaps let malaria return. Elimination needs surveillance as much as spraying.

0:03–0:08. Check the NSP 2023–27 PDF on the NCVBDC website for the current targets and categories.

Malaria Elimination · Diagnosis

Test Every Fever

Microscopy

Thick and thin smear: the gold standard; species, stage, parasite load. Result within 24 hours.

Bivalent RDT

Detects Pf (HRP-2) and pan or Pv antigen. Used by ASHAs in villages; result in 15–20 minutes.

No treatment without a test, except presumptive treatment when severe malaria is suspected and testing is not possible.

0:13–0:20. HRP-2 stays positive for weeks after cure, so a positive RDT soon after treatment does not mean failure. pfhrp2 gene deletions can cause false negatives.

Malaria Elimination · Drug Policy

Treat by Species

No primaquine in pregnancy, infants under 1 year, or known G6PD deficiency. First trimester: quinine per national policy.

0:20–0:30. Primaquine single dose in falciparum kills gametocytes (stops transmission); the 14-day course in vivax kills hypnozoites (prevents relapse). WHO now allows artemether-lumefantrine in the first trimester (2022); check whether national policy has been updated.

Malaria Elimination · Vector Control

Integrated Vector Management

Indoor Residual Spraying

Two rounds before peak season in high-risk villages; insecticide chosen by local resistance (synthetic pyrethroids, malathion)

Long-lasting Insecticidal Nets

Last about 3 years; target every sleeper; effective only if used every night

Larval Source Management

Drain, fill, cover; temephos; larvivorous fish (Gambusia, guppy) in tanks and wells

People

Repellents, screens, full sleeves; behaviour change communication; inter-sector work (mining, irrigation, urban bodies)

0:35–0:47. Widespread DDT resistance in An. culicifacies drove the shift to other insecticides. Bio-environmental methods suit urban areas with An. stephensi.

Malaria Elimination · Surveillance

In Elimination, Every Case Is an Event

Find

Active (ASHA, MPW house visits) and passive (facility) case detection; mass screening in hot spots

Investigate

Indigenous or imported? Map the case; screen household and neighbours

Respond

Focus investigation and response: treatment, vector control, follow-up

0:47–0:52. China's 1-3-7 approach (report in 1 day, investigate in 3, respond in 7) is a useful model to discuss. Real-time reporting is through the IHIP portal.

Malaria Elimination · Take-Home

Take-Home

  1. Goal: malaria-free India by 2030.
  2. Test every fever: smear or bivalent RDT.
  3. Vivax: CQ + 14-day PQ. Falciparum: ACT + PQ day 2.
  4. IRS, LLINs, larval control: match to the vector.
  5. Elimination runs on case-based surveillance.
