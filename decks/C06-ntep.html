NTEP · DS-TB Regimen

Drug-sensitive TB: 2HRZE / 4HRE, Daily

  • Intensive phase: 2 months of isoniazid, rifampicin, pyrazinamide, ethambutol
  • Continuation phase: 4 months of HRE (can be extended)
  • Daily fixed-dose combinations by weight band

tablets of 4FDC daily in the intensive phase; the same number of 3FDC (HRE) in the continuation phase

0:11–0:20. Adult bands: 25–34 kg: 2; 35–49 kg: 3; 50–64 kg: 4; 65–75 kg: 5; above 75 kg: 6. Paediatric dispersible FDCs have separate bands. Verify against the current Central TB Division technical guidance.

NTEP · DR-TB

Drug-resistant TB: Shorter and All-oral Now

BPaLM · 6 months

Bedaquiline, pretomanid, linezolid, moxifloxacin for MDR/RR-TB (age 14 and above), introduced in India in 2024

Shorter Oral · 9–11 months

Bedaquiline-containing regimen for eligible patients

Longer · 18–20 months

Individualised, for extensive resistance or those not eligible for shorter regimens

MDR-TB: resistant to at least isoniazid and rifampicin. Pre-XDR adds fluoroquinolone resistance; XDR adds bedaquiline or linezolid as well.

NTEP · TB Preventive Treatment

TB Preventive Treatment (TPT)

Who Gets TPT
  • PLHIV (adults and children)
  • Household contacts of pulmonary TB: all ages (testing for infection where available for those 5 and above)
  • Other risk groups: on dialysis, anti-TNF, transplant, silicosis

Always rule out active TB first.

Regimens

6H: daily isoniazid for 6 months
3HP: weekly isoniazid + rifapentine for 3 months (12 doses)
For contacts of MDR-TB: levofloxacin (6Lfx)

NTEP · Patient Support

Support That Keeps Patients on Treatment

Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana

₹1,000 a month by direct benefit transfer for the duration of treatment (raised from ₹500 in 2024)

Ni-kshay Mitra

Individuals and organisations adopt patients and give monthly food baskets

Ni-kshay Portal

Case notification, follow-up, payments, adherence technology

Treatment Supporter

A trained person close to the patient; honorarium on completion

Plus free diagnosis and drugs in public and private sectors, incentives for private providers to notify, and screening for HIV and diabetes in every TB patient.

NTEP · Take-Home

Take-Home

  1. NTEP (2020): Detect–Treat–Prevent–Build.

  3. MDR/RR-TB: BPaLM for 6 months where eligible.
